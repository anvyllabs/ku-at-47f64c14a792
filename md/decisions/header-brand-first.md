# ADR: Header is brand-first

Date: 2026-11-10
Decision: Put “AnvylTube by Anvyl Labs” at the top, with “BUSS215-06·07 · Prof. KyungBae Park” underneath.
Why: The story we tell is: Andrej Karpathy built HackerTube from open data, Anvyl Labs tried the same approach, and now it's your team's turn. Sources are credited from the start.
Alternatives: A course-first header (rejected by the instructor).
Consequence: The page has no sales copy, contact buttons, or student data collection. Anvyl Labs appears only as the team that tried it.
